## Entry Points for Contributors

**Start anywhere. Fork freely. No permission needed.**

### Story Entry Points

- Write a scene from the perspective of the stolen white pine itself, from its life in the forest to its silent, stacked imprisonment in the rail yard.
- Create a monologue for Hiram Morgan, defending his 'progress' against Silas's accusations of sensory theft.
- Write a chapter where a secondary character—the young clerk, Captain O'Shaughnessy—first becomes aware of the 'silence' or 'sigh' that Silas perceives.
- Imagine a modern-day sequel: a descendant of Silas Thorne uses bioacoustic monitoring in a threatened rainforest, hearing a new kind of industrial 'silence'.

### Technical Entry Points

- Build an interactive map tracing Silas's journey upriver, layering period-correct sounds (river, industry, forest) that change as he travels.
- Create a data sonification project that translates Gilded Age economic data (timber output, rail mileage) into an audio landscape, contrasting 'industrial' and 'natural' soundscapes.
